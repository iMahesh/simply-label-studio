Is SimplyLabel Studio really free?

Yes. SimplyLabel Studio is free to use, with no account required.

Can I use it on a Mac?

The editor runs as a desktop Chrome extension, including on macOS. Printing uses your computer’s print system, so your printer still needs a working driver and matching paper setup.

Will it work with my label printer?

It prints through Chrome’s normal print dialog. Use a printer supported by your computer, select the matching paper size, set scaling to 100%, and print one test label first. We don’t promise universal printer compatibility.

Can I save my labels and move them to another computer?

Yes. Save editable project files or export your presets as a JSON backup, then import them on another computer. Browser-local presets don’t automatically sync between devices.

Can I save a label as a PDF?

Choose Save as PDF in Chrome’s print dialog. Editable projects and preset backups are saved separately as JSON files.

Are the QR codes dynamic?

No. QR codes store the text or URL you enter. Managed redirects and scan analytics aren’t included. Changing a printed code’s destination requires a new code unless you control the destination website itself.

Which barcode formats are supported?

The current editor generates CODE 128 barcodes with an optional readable caption. UPC, EAN, and GS1 formats aren’t included. Scan a printed test with your intended device before using a batch.

Privacy at a glance

This website has no analytics, tracking scripts, contact forms, or account system.

Inside the extension

Label drafts, presets, text, date settings, and selected images are processed locally in your browser profile. The editor does not send label content to a developer server. You choose when to export a project, back up presets, or pass a label to Chrome and your selected printer or PDF destination.

Keeping your work

Export backups before clearing browser data or removing the extension. Sharing a project or preset backup also shares the text and images saved in it.

This is an overview of the editor’s behavior, not the completed publisher privacy policy. The final policy and support contact will be linked here at launch. Your chosen website host may process technical request data under its own policies.

Your first test print

  1. Set the label dimensions in the editor to match your media.
  2. Choose Print, then Print one test.
  3. Select your printer or Save as PDF in Chrome.
  4. Choose matching paper dimensions, 100% scale, and headers and footers off.
  5. Use one copy in the driver dialog; the editor generates your requested batch quantity.
  6. Check for clipping, blank trailing pages, and readable small text. Scan any printed QR codes or barcodes with your intended device.

Print results depend on your printer driver, calibration, and media. Keep backups of your saved designs.
